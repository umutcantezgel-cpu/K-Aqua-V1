# Socket

PP-R socket for joining two pipes of equal nominal size by polyfusion.
A central stop limits the insertion depth of both pipe ends.

## Dimension key

| Symbol | Meaning |
|---|---|
| d | pipe outside diameter = socket bore |
| D | outside diameter of the socket |
| l | overall length |
| z | thickness of the central stop |

Fusion depth follows from the table: (l − z)/2.
