Modern building services engineering places increasingly high demands on the materials used. Particularly in drinking water and heating installations, conventional PP-R is often no longer sufficient to meet the heightened requirements for pressure, temperature, and longevity. This is where PP-RCT (Polypropylene Random Copolymer with modified Crystallinity and Temperature resistance) comes into play.

## What exactly is PP-RCT and how does it work?

PP-RCT (Polypropylene Random Copolymer with modified Crystallinity and Temperature resistance) represents the fourth generation of polypropylene piping materials. Unlike classic PP-R (Type 3), where the crystal structure is distributed irregularly, PP-RCT (Type 4) is produced through a special, nucleated polymerization process.

This process creates particularly fine and evenly distributed crystals within the polymer matrix. This altered morphology gives the material a significantly higher level of performance without changing the excellent chemical properties of the base material.

### The Crystalline Structure as a Gamechanger
The modified crystallinity means that stresses in the material under the influence of pressure and temperature are dissipated much more evenly. The result is a significantly flatter stress regression curve at elevated temperatures. Simply put: The material fatigues much more slowly in hot water under high pressure than conventional plastics.

## The 4 Decisive Advantages in Detail

### 1. Higher Pressure Resistance with Reduced Wall Thickness
Due to the improved mechanical strength of PP-RCT, pipes can be extruded with thinner walls for the same nominal pressure (PN) (e.g., SDR 9 or SDR 11 instead of SDR 6). 
This has massive hydraulic effects:
* **Larger Internal Diameter**: With the same outer diameter, the pipe offers up to 37% more flow capacity.
* **Smaller Dimensioning Possible**: Often, due to the improved hydraulics, the entire piping system can be designed one dimension smaller, which saves material costs and space in the installation shaft.
* **Weight Reduction**: Thinner walls mean less material used (up to 25% less weight), which enormously facilitates handling on the construction site, especially with large dimensions suspended from the ceiling.

### 2. Excellent Longevity Under Extreme Conditions (50+ Years)
K-Aqua piping systems made of PP-RCT are designed for a minimum service life of 50 years – even at a constant 70°C and 10 bar pressure. They not only withstand the daily temperature fluctuations in heating and cooling circuits but also excellently absorb pressure surges (water hammer) in the line due to their inherent flexibility.

### 3. Absolute Freedom from Corrosion and Incrustation
Unlike metallic systems, PP-RCT pipes are physically unable to rust. Pitting corrosion caused by aggressive water qualities or galvanic corrosion between different metals is also completely ruled out. The microscopically smooth inner surface (roughness of only 0.007 mm) also reliably prevents incrustations (calcium deposits). The pipe cross-section remains unchanged for decades, which keeps the pump performance in the system permanently at the optimal level.

### 4. Outstanding Thermal and Acoustic Insulation
PP-RCT has a very low thermal conductivity (0.24 W/mK). This means that hot water pipes radiate less heat to the environment, and cold water pipes form less condensation than metal pipes. In addition, the material has strong sound-insulating properties. Flow noises and pressure shocks are absorbed by the pipe itself, leading to a significantly quieter drinking water installation in the building.

## Seamless, Homogeneous Connection Technology

Another huge advantage for the installer: The connection technology for PP-RCT remains identical to classic PP-R. The pipes and fittings are permanently connected to one another by polyfusion welding (socket welding or butt welding) at 260°C. 

In this process, the pipe and fitting fuse at the molecular level into a single, homogeneous workpiece with no weak points or sealing rings that could become porous over time. For the installer, this means: No new tools, no expensive press jaws, but a system with maximum security.
