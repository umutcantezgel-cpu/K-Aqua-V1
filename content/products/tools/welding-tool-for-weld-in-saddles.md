The safe and durable installation of piping systems requires highly specialized and precise tools. Our **Welding Tool for Weld-in Saddles** has been meticulously developed for the professional processing and joining of saddle fittings onto main pipes. It guarantees a perfectly sealed, materially bonded, and highly resilient welded connection in modern plumbing and pipeline technology. This specific heating tool is an indispensable instrument for plant engineers, pipeline installers, and professionals working with complex plastic pipe systems (e.g., PP-R or PE) in cold and hot water applications, industrial systems, or heating networks.

#### Functionality and Technical Superiority

The special welding tool serves as a heating element designed to melt both the outer wall of the main pipe and the inner surface of the weld-in saddle (the branch) simultaneously, evenly, and under precise temperature specifications. Once the optimal melting temperature is reached (typically between 250 and 260 °C for PP-R), the two components are joined accurately and swiftly. The result is a homogeneous and extremely durable molecular connection that requires no additional sealants, O-rings, or mechanical fixings.

The precisely calibrated diameters of the tool prevent excess material from accumulating inside the pipe during the welding process, ensuring that the internal flow cross-section is not restricted. The tool features a high-quality, non-stick coated surface (usually PTFE / Teflon). This premium coating prevents the molten plastic from sticking to the tool, makes cleaning effortless, and ensures consistently high-quality welds over a long lifespan.

#### Comprehensive Range for Every Pipe Diameter

To meet all structural requirements and piping dimensions, we offer the Welding Tool for Weld-in Saddles in a wide variety of sizes. The dimensions refer to the range of the main pipe (Pipe Range) and the diameter of the outgoing saddle branch (Branch d). Our product portfolio includes:

*   **Main Pipe 40–63 mm:** Branch diameter of 25 mm (Article Code AQ9850400632S). Ideal for smaller distribution lines and floor-level installations.

#### Application Areas and Practical Advantages

Using weld-in saddles instead of classic, bulky Tee-pieces saves a tremendous amount of space, material, and labor time. This technique is particularly advantageous for creating subsequent branches on existing lines or constructing complex manifolds in tight installation shafts. By utilizing our specialized welding tool, you benefit from:
1.  **Time Savings:** There is no need to completely cut and interrupt the main pipe. The saddle is welded directly onto the pipe wall (after drilling the required hole).
2.  **Cost Efficiency:** Using saddles reduces material costs significantly compared to purchasing large-dimension Tee-fittings.
3.  **High Operational Safety:** The fusion-welded joint is highly pressure-resistant, temperature-resistant, and absolutely free of corrosion.
4.  **Maximum Flexibility:** Branches can be positioned exactly where they are needed, without being restricted by the fixed dimensions of standard fittings.
