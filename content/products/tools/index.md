Von der ersten Rohrabtrennung bis hin zur finalen Schweißverbindung deckt K-Aqua jeden Schritt des Installationsprozesses ab. Egal ob Muffenschweißen (Socket Fusion), Stumpfschweißen (Butt Fusion) oder das Elektromuffenschweißen (Electrofusion) – wir bieten für jede Technik die passenden, langlebigen Maschinen und Zubehörteile.

### Hochwertige Rohrscheren für den perfekten Schnitt (Pipe Cutters)

Ein sauberer, rechtwinkliger und gratfreier Schnitt ist die Grundvoraussetzung für jede erfolgreiche Schweißung. Die K-Aqua Rohrscheren (Pipe Cutters) sind in verschiedenen Größen, beispielsweise für die Durchmesser 20–40 mm und 50–125 mm, erhältlich. Ihre scharfen, spezialgehärteten Klingen durchtrennen das PP-R Material mühelos und ohne Verformung des Rohres. 

- Geringer Kraftaufwand: Durch eine optimierte Hebelübersetzung und Ratschenmechanik lässt sich der Schnittvorgang kraftschonend durchführen.
- Präzision: Die Konstruktion der Rohrschere führt die Klinge exakt im 90-Grad-Winkel durch das Rohr, was ein späteres, nahtloses Einschmelzen in die Muffe garantiert.
- Robustheit: Entwickelt für den harten Baustellenalltag, überzeugen die Scheren durch Langlebigkeit und minimale Wartungsanforderungen.

### Muffenschweißen (Socket Fusion): Hand- und Tischschweißmaschinen

Das Heizelement-Muffenschweißen ist die am häufigsten angewandte Verbindungstechnik für PP-R Rohre im Durchmesserbereich von 20 mm bis 125 mm. K-Aqua bietet hierfür komplette Hand-Schweißgeräte (Hand Welding Machines) für die Dimensionen 20–32 mm und 20–63 mm an. Für größere Dimensionen von 50–125 mm stehen leistungsstarke Schweißmaschinen zur Verfügung, die eine gleichmäßige und kontrollierte Erwärmung sicherstellen.

- Temperaturkontrolle: Eine hochpräzise Thermostat- oder Elektronikregelung hält die Schweißtemperatur konstant im optimalen Bereich (in der Regel bei 260°C). 
- Teflon-beschichtete Schweißwerkzeuge (Welding Tools): Die Heizelemente (Matrizen und Dorne) sind mit einer hochwertigen Antihaftbeschichtung (PTFE) versehen. Dies verhindert das Anhaften des aufgeschmolzenen Kunststoffs, erleichtert die Reinigung und sorgt für perfekte, homogene Schweißnähte ohne Einschlüsse.
- Ergonomie: Handgeräte sind so ausbalanciert, dass sie auch bei Überkopf-Arbeiten oder an schwer zugänglichen Stellen sicher und ermüdungsfrei geführt werden können.

### Stumpfschweißen (Butt Fusion) für große Dimensionen

Wenn es um Hauptverteilungsleitungen oder Steigleitungen mit großen Durchmessern geht, ist das Stumpfschweißen oft die Methode der Wahl. Die K-Aqua Stumpfschweißmaschine (Butt Welding Machine) für Durchmesser von 90 mm bis 250 mm ist ein robustes Kraftpaket für Industrie- und Großprojekte.

- Stabile Grundmaschine: Die Konstruktion bietet auch bei langen und schweren Rohren einen sicheren Halt und verhindert ein Verrutschen während des Schweißvorgangs.

### Professional K-Aqua Installation Tools for PP-R Pipe Systems: Precision, Reliability, and Maximum Efficiency on Site

The proper and professional installation of PP-R (Polypropylene Random Copolymer) piping systems requires more than just high-quality pipes and fittings—it demands the right, high-precision tools. To guarantee completely leak-proof, long-lasting, and hygienically flawless connections, welding processes must be executed exactly according to established industry standards. This is precisely where K-Aqua installation tools come into play. Our comprehensive range of specialized tools is perfectly matched to the unique requirements of K-Aqua PP-R systems, providing professionals on construction sites with the safety, consistency, and reliability indispensable for demanding plumbing, heating, and air conditioning installations.

From the very first pipe cut to the final fused joint, K-Aqua covers every single step of the installation workflow. Whether you are dealing with socket fusion, butt fusion, or electrofusion welding techniques, we offer the appropriate, durable machines and accessories to get the job done right the first time.

### High-Quality Pipe Cutters for the Perfect Cut

A clean, perfectly square, and burr-free cut is the foundational requirement for any successful plastic pipe weld. The K-Aqua pipe cutters are available in various sizes to accommodate different pipe diameters, such as the 20–40 mm and 50–125 mm ranges. Their razor-sharp, specially hardened steel blades slice through the tough PP-R material effortlessly, without deforming or crushing the pipe.

- Reduced Effort: Thanks to an optimized lever action and a smooth ratcheting mechanism, the cutting process is physically undemanding, reducing installer fatigue during long shifts.
- Absolute Precision: The design of the pipe cutter guides the blade at a strict 90-degree angle through the pipe, which guarantees a seamless insertion into the heated socket later on.
- Exceptional Durability: Engineered for the harsh realities of daily construction site use, these cutters impress with their long lifespan and minimal maintenance requirements.

### Socket Fusion: Hand and Bench Welding Machines

Socket fusion (or thermal polyfusion) is the most frequently used connection method for PP-R pipes in the diameter range from 20 mm up to 125 mm. K-Aqua provides complete Hand Welding Machines for sizes 20–32 mm and 20–63 mm. For larger dimensions, ranging from 50–125 mm, powerful bench-style welding machines are available to ensure uniform and controlled heating of the larger surface areas.

- Precise Temperature Control: High-accuracy thermostatic or electronic controllers keep the welding temperature constantly within the optimal range (typically exactly at 260°C or 500°F).
- Teflon-Coated Welding Tools (Dies): The heating elements (spigots and sockets/matrices) are coated with a premium non-stick polytetrafluoroethylene (PTFE) layer. This critical feature prevents melted plastic from adhering to the metal, facilitates rapid cleaning, and ensures perfectly homogeneous welds without any material inclusions.
- Ergonomic Handling: The manual hand tools are carefully balanced so they can be guided safely and comfortably, even when performing overhead work or operating in tight, hard-to-reach plumbing shafts.

### Butt Fusion Machines for Large Diameters

When working with main distribution lines, large risers, or industrial chilled water lines with significant diameters, butt fusion is often the method of choice. The K-Aqua Butt Welding Machine, designed for pipe diameters ranging from 90 mm up to 250 mm, is a robust powerhouse built specifically for industrial and large-scale commercial projects.

- Hydraulic or Mechanical Control: The joining pressure can be adjusted with pinpoint accuracy and maintained constantly throughout the critical cooling phase.
- Integrated Planer and Heating Plate: An electrical facing tool (planer) ensures the pipe ends are absolutely parallel and smooth before the high-precision heating plate plasticizes the pipe ends.
- Stable Base Frame: The heavy-duty machine chassis provides a secure grip, preventing long, heavy pipes from shifting or slipping during the delicate welding procedure.

### Electrofusion for Maximum Flexibility

For complex repairs, post-installation extensions, or working in deeply confined spaces where traditional machines cannot fit, electrofusion welding is the ultimate solution. The K-Aqua Electrofusion Machine acts as an intelligent control unit that monitors the entire fusion process fully automatically.

- Barcode Recognition: By simply scanning the barcode printed directly on the electrofusion fitting, the machine automatically downloads the correct welding parameters, including voltage and fusion time.
- Data Logging: Modern electrofusion control units store the welding data, enabling seamless documentation and quality assurance reporting for the project owner or inspector.
- Enhanced Safety: Visual and acoustic signals keep the operator informed about the progress and successful completion of the weld.

### Specialized Tools: Weld-in Saddles and Repairing Plugs

Beyond standard joints, plumbing installations frequently require the addition of branches after the fact, or the rapid repair of accidental drilling damage. K-Aqua offers specialized tools exactly for these scenarios:

- Weld-in Saddles: Using the matching Drilling Tools and specific heating dies for saddles, installers can efficiently melt new branch connections into existing main pipelines without having to cut the main pipe completely and insert a traditional tee fitting.
- Repairing Plugs: If a pipe is accidentally punctured by a drill on site, the K-Aqua Welding Tool for Repairing Plug and the corresponding repair pins provide a rapid, permanently sealed solution, avoiding costly and time-consuming wall demolitions and full pipe replacements.
