Das Elektroschweißverfahren (Electrofusion) ist eine der sichersten und effizientesten Methoden, um Kunststoffrohre dauerhaft miteinander zu verbinden. Im Gegensatz zu herkömmlichen Methoden, bei denen Rohrenden erhitzt und mechanisch zusammengepresst werden, verwendet die Elektroschweißtechnik spezielle Fittings mit integrierten Heizwendeln. Sobald elektrischer Strom durch diese Wendeln fließt, schmilzt der Kunststoff an den Kontaktflächen von Fitting und Rohr, wodurch eine homogene und unlösbare Materialverbindung entsteht. #### The Importance of Electrofusion Technology
Electrofusion is universally recognized as one of the safest, most efficient, and most reliable methods for permanently joining plastic pipes. Unlike traditional butt-welding methods that involve physically heating pipe ends and pressing them together mechanically, electrofusion utilizes specialized fittings embedded with internal heating coils. When an electrical current is precisely delivered through these coils, the plastic material at the interface of the fitting and the pipe melts, creating a homogeneous, inseparable bond upon cooling. Our Electrofusion Machine acts as the intelligent brain behind this process, meticulously monitoring and controlling the electrical output to guarantee a 100% flawless weld every single time.

#### Barcode-Scanning for Maximum Process Efficiency
A standout feature of the K-Aqua AQ990 Electrofusion Machine is its integrated **barcode-scanning functionality**. Every high-quality electrofusion fitting comes with a specific barcode that encodes all the essential welding parameters, including the required voltage, precise heating time, and the necessary cooling period. By simply scanning this barcode with the attached scanner, the installer transfers all critical data directly and automatically to the control unit. This completely eliminates the risk of human error associated with manual data entry, significantly speeds up the workflow on busy construction sites, and ensures that every single fitting is welded exactly according to the manufacturer's strict specifications. In large-scale infrastructural projects involving hundreds or thousands of joints, this feature alone translates to massive time and cost savings.

#### Automatic Welding Parameter Control
Beyond simple barcode reading, this control unit is equipped with advanced **automatic welding parameter control**. Utilizing sophisticated microprocessor technology, the machine continuously monitors ambient environmental conditions, such as temperature, as well as real-time power consumption during the entire welding cycle. If there are fluctuations in the power supply or extreme weather conditions (such as freezing cold or intense heat), the machine dynamically adjusts the energy output in real-time. This active compensation mechanism ensures that the melting process remains perfectly optimal regardless of external variables. The result is consistently high weld quality that meets and exceeds the most rigorous international standards for pressurized piping networks.
