### Drilling Tool for Weld-in Saddle: Precision and Efficiency in Pipe Branching

The Drilling Tool for Weld-in Saddle is an indispensable specialized tool for modern pipeline construction, used specifically after the welding of plastic saddles. When you need to create reliable and precise branches in existing or newly laid pipe networks, this core drill tool provides the exact solution to open the branch connection cleanly and professionally.

Integrating weld-in saddles is one of the safest and most durable methods for creating branches on PE (Polyethylene) or PP (Polypropylene) pipes. Following a successful welding process, the main pipe must be punctured at the specific location to allow fluid or gas to flow into the new branch. This is exactly where our drilling tool excels. It ensures a clean cut, minimizes swarf generation, and prevents any damage to the newly welded joint.

#### Technical Specifications and Model Variants

To meet diverse field requirements, we offer the Drilling Tool for Weld-in Saddle in various dimensions, perfectly matched to the most common pipe diameters. Each tool is manufactured from high-strength, wear-resistant materials to guarantee a long service life and consistent drilling performance, even under intensive use on construction sites.

Our product range includes the following models (including article codes and diameter `d` in mm):
- AQ98625: Diameter 25 mm – ideal for smaller domestic connections and fine branches.
- AQ98632: Diameter 32 mm – a common size for standard water lines in building technology.
- AQ98640: Diameter 40 mm – perfect for medium flow rates in commercial and private networks.
- AQ98650: Diameter 50 mm – for larger branches in municipal or industrial supply lines.
- AQ98663: Diameter 63 mm – for large main lines and high volume flows.

All tools are designed to ensure optimal chip and swarf removal during the drilling process. This is of critical importance to prevent plastic residues from entering the piping system, where they could potentially block valves, pumps, or water meters.

#### Areas of Application and Use Cases

The field of application for the Drilling Tool for Weld-in Saddle is extremely diverse. It is primarily used in the following sectors:
1. Drinking Water Supply: When connecting new buildings to the existing water network under pressure, or during subsequent infrastructure expansions.
2. Gas Supply: For highly secure branches on gas pipelines, where an absolutely leak-proof weld and a precise, burr-free hole are vital.
3. Industrial Piping Systems: In chemical and processing plants where plastic piping (like PE-HD or PP) is used, and additional branches for sensors or secondary lines are required.
4. Irrigation Technology: For large-scale agricultural irrigation systems where main distributors are equipped with numerous secondary lines.
5. Wastewater and Geothermal Energy: Wherever leak-proof and durable connections are required underground.

#### Advantages of the Specialized Drilling Tool

Using our specifically designed tool offers significant advantages over conventional drills:
- Burr-free Opening: The specially ground cutting edges ensure an extremely clean cut. Post-processing or deburring is generally not required.
- Controlled Drilling Process: The exact guidance prevents the drilling tool from penetrating too deeply and damaging the opposite wall of the pipe.
- Core Retention: The design of the drill allows the cut-out plastic core (the so-called "plug") to be held securely within the tool, so it can be easily removed after drilling and does not fall into the pipe network.
- Ergonomics and Handling: The tool can be operated both manually and with power tools, which significantly reduces the workload on the construction site.
- Compatibility: It is exactly matched to the dimensions of our weld-in saddles, thus guaranteeing a flawless fit and maximum tightness.

#### Installation Instructions for Professionals

For optimal results, the drilling process should only be carried out after the saddle weld has completely cooled and cured. It is imperative to observe the cooling times specified by the manufacturer. Place the drilling tool absolutely straight onto the branch socket. Apply even, not excessive pressure, and let the cutting edges do the work. When working on pressurized lines ("hot tapping"), appropriate safety gate valves and tapping machines must be used.

In summary, the Drilling Tool for Weld-in Saddle is a crucial factor for the safety, efficiency, and longevity of your pipe installations. It represents the highest tool quality for professional users who refuse to compromise on their piping networks.
