#### Technical Specifications and Advanced Design Features

This specific model is a full-bore ball valve, meticulously engineered to reduce pressure drop within the system to an absolute minimum. A full-bore (or full-port) design means that the internal diameter of the ball precisely matches the internal diameter of the connected pipe. This vital feature prevents cavitation, minimizes turbulent flow noises, and ensures an completely unrestricted flow of media, thereby maximizing the hydraulic efficiency of the entire network.

The choice of materials is the true heart of this product:
1. The Body and the Core (Ball) are manufactured from premium-grade PP-R. This material is renowned for its exceptional chemical resistance and is completely food-safe and non-toxic, making it the perfect choice for the transport of potable water (both hot and cold).

#### Installation Methodology: The Socket Fusion Process

Another massive advantage of the PP-R Ball Valve with PP Core is its highly secure and straightforward installation process. The valve is connected directly to the PP-R pipe using the socket fusion welding method. During this process, both the exterior of the pipe end and the interior of the valve socket are simultaneously heated using a specialized PTFE-coated heating tool to the exact melting temperature of the plastic (approximately 260 °C / 500 °F). Once the melting point is reached, the pipe is pushed into the valve socket.

As the components cool down, the polymer chains of the pipe and the valve fuse together, creating a single, uniform piece of material. There are no mechanical threads that could loosen, no rubber O-rings that degrade over time, and no need for sealing tapes or pastes. This fusion technique is not only remarkably fast to execute but also provides unparalleled security against leaks, which is invaluable—especially when pipes and valves are concealed behind walls or under floors.

Furthermore, the extreme longevity of the system—often exceeding a 50-year service life—promotes resource conservation. Because the material does not corrode or scale, premature replacements are eliminated, drastically reducing waste and lifecycle maintenance costs.
