### Technical Specifications and Performance Features in Detail

The technical parameters of the K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 11 speak for themselves. At an operating temperature of 20°C, the pipe withstands a working pressure of up to 1.6 MPa (16 bar). At elevated temperatures of 60°C, typical for hot water and heating circuits, the maximum pressure load is still an impressive 0.8 MPa (8 bar). These performance values make the K-Fiber pipe a highly reliable component for cold and hot water installations as well as for heating systems.

A key feature of this piping system is the integrated fiber composite middle layer. This layer significantly reduces linear thermal expansion compared to conventional PP-R pipes without glass fiber reinforcement. This means that fewer expansion loops and mounting brackets are required to compensate for temperature fluctuations, which not only saves material costs but also drastically reduces installation time. Furthermore, the fiber layer increases the mechanical strength and rigidity of the pipe, allowing for secure and straight routing over longer distances.
