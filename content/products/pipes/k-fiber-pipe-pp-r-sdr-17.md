#### In-Depth Technical Specifications and Performance Data

The K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 17 is defined by its impressive technical specifications, making it a reliable choice for engineers and contractors. It is classified under the pipe series S 8 and features a Standard Dimension Ratio of SDR 17. This precise classification guarantees outstanding performance across various operating conditions. The available dimensions highlight the product's focus on large-scale projects and main distribution lines. #### Fiber Composite Technology: A Decisive Advantage

The true core and primary advantage of the K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 17 is its innovative fiber composite layer. Standard PP-R pipes have a natural tendency to exhibit significant linear expansion when subjected to temperature fluctuations. By integrating specially formulated glass fibers into the middle layer of the pipe during the extrusion process, this linear expansion is drastically reduced—often by up to 75% when compared to standard, unreinforced plastic pipes. In practical application, this translates to several major benefits:

1. Fewer Support Requirements: Due to the significantly increased inherent rigidity and the vastly reduced thermal expansion, the required distances between pipe clamps and supports can be noticeably extended. This saves substantial amounts of material, reduces labor time, and ultimately lowers overall installation costs.
2. Higher Flow Capacity: Because of the thinner wall thickness (SDR 17) relative to thicker-walled pipes of the same outer diameter, this pipe provides a larger internal diameter. This directly results in a significantly higher flow volume and substantially reduced pressure losses, improving system efficiency.
3. Exceptional Form Stability: The pipe maintains its rigid, straight form even at higher operating temperatures and does not sag, which is absolutely essential for maintaining a clean, professional, and structurally sound installation.
