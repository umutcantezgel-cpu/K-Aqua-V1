#### Innovative Glass Fiber Technology for Maximum Structural Stability

The core strength of the K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 7.4 lies in its advanced material composition. The middle layer, reinforced with high-tensile glass fibers, significantly reduces the linear thermal expansion of the pipe. Compared to conventional solid plastic pipes, the K-Fiber Pipe exhibits drastically less expansion and contraction when subjected to temperature fluctuations. This not only minimizes the need for complex expansion loops and compensators but also vastly simplifies system design and installation. Concurrently, the reinforced structure reduces pipe sagging, allowing for greater distances between support brackets, which ultimately saves both material and labor costs. The inner and outer PP-R layers provide a perfectly smooth, hygienic surface that effectively prevents scaling, encrustation, and bacterial growth.

#### Outstanding Pressure and Temperature Resistance

Engineered with an SDR 7.4 (Standard Dimension Ratio) and classified under pipe series S 3.2, the K-Fiber Pipe is meticulously designed to withstand rigorous pressure and temperature profiles. At a standard operating temperature of 20°C, the system can reliably handle an impressive working pressure of up to 2.5 MPa (25 bar). Even under elevated temperature conditions of 60°C – which are typical for domestic hot water distribution or central heating systems – the pipe maintains a robust pressure resistance of 1.2 MPa (12 bar). These exceptional specifications make the K-Fiber Pipe the ideal choice for demanding hot water networks, chilled water air conditioning circuits, and industrial fluid transport where consistent, uncompromising performance under extreme conditions is absolutely mandatory.

The product portfolio includes pipes with outer diameters of 20 mm, 25 mm, 32 mm, 40 mm, and 50 mm. The corresponding wall thicknesses, ranging from 2.8 mm to 6.9 mm, are precisely calibrated to each dimension to ensure the optimal balance between maximum flow capacity and structural integrity. Supplied in highly practical 4-meter straight lengths, the system can be efficiently stored, transported, and handled on-site. The remarkably low weight of the pipe – for instance, merely 0.16 kg/m for the 20 mm variant – greatly facilitates handling, reducing physical strain on installers and speeding up the construction workflow.

In an era of increasing environmental awareness and stringent green building standards, the K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 7.4 excels in sustainability. The installation of the K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 7.4 is executed using the highly reliable process of polyfusion welding.
