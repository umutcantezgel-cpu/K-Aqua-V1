## SEO-CONTENT-EN
### K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 6 — glass-fibre reinforced PP-R rated 2.0 MPa at 20 °C

The K-Fiber Pipe PP-R SDR 6 is a glass-fibre reinforced PP-R GF pipe in ten
nominal sizes from d20 to d125, supplied in 4-metre bars. Sizes d20 to d63 are
also available in 5.80-metre lengths on request, ordered as AQ258PF plus the
dimension.

#### Pressure rating and wall thickness
At SDR 6 (pipe series S 2.5) the pipe is rated 2.0 MPa at 20 °C and 1.0 MPa at
70 °C. Minimum wall thickness ranges from 3.4 mm at d20 to 20.8 mm at d125,
making it the thickest-walled pipe in the PP-R K-Fiber range.

#### Glass-fibre core layer
The middle layer is glass-fibre reinforced. The pipe is green with four red
stripes, the marking of the fibre range.

#### Standards
The pipe conforms to DIN EN ISO 15874 and DIN 8077 / 8078.
