### K-Aqua PP-R Flange Adaptor (Stub End): Uncompromising Reliability for Your Piping Systems
The K-Aqua PP-R Flange Adaptor, commonly known in the industry as a Stub End, is a fundamentally crucial component for constructing highly durable, pressure-resistant, and completely corrosion-free piping networks. This specially engineered PP-R (Polypropylene Random Copolymer) fitting serves as the perfect transition element, allowing you to securely and leak-proof connect plastic pipes to flanged heavy equipment, such as gate valves, industrial pumps, water meters, and metallic pipe sections. In modern building services engineering, industrial plant construction, and municipal water supply, such transitions are critical stress points – the K-Aqua Flange Adaptor handles these challenges effortlessly.

#### Technical Specifications and Advanced Design Features
The structural design of the K-Aqua Stub End is meticulously optimized for maximum mechanical strength, load-bearing capacity, and thermal stability. The perfectly smooth inner surface of the PP-R material guarantees minimal friction losses and effectively prevents the formation of any scale, deposits, or biological encrustations. This smooth bore significantly enhances the overall lifespan and flow efficiency of the entire pipeline system.
Our Flange Adaptors are available in a comprehensive range of dimensions to meet all project requirements:
* **Available Outer Diameters (d):** 40 mm, 50 mm, 63 mm, 75 mm, 90 mm, 110 mm, and 125 mm.
* **Nominal Diameters (DN):** Precisely matched from DN 40 to DN 125 to ensure seamless and standardized integration into universal flanged systems.
* **Article Codes:** Ranging from AQ79040 up to AQ790125.
The PP-R material is globally renowned for its exceptional chemical resistance. As a result, the K-Aqua flange adaptor is perfectly suited not only for potable water but also for a wide array of demanding industrial media, including highly aggressive fluids, acidic solutions, and alkaline chemicals.

#### The Socket Fusion Welding Process
The K-Aqua PP-R Stub End is permanently joined to the corresponding PP-R pipe using the highly reliable socket fusion welding process. During this thermofusion procedure, the outer surface of the pipe and the inner surface of the fitting are simultaneously heated and melted. Once joined, they fuse together to form a homogeneous, inseparable, and cohesive material bond.
Advantages of Socket Fusion with K-Aqua:
1. **Absolute Leak-Tightness:** Unlike threaded or chemically glued joints, homogenous thermal fusion completely eliminates the risk of micro-leaks, ensuring zero water loss over the system's lifetime.
2. **Rapid Installation:** The fusion process is incredibly time-efficient. Trained technicians can perform these welds quickly and precisely, even under challenging construction site conditions.
3. **High Pressure and Temperature Resistance:** The welded joint is frequently stronger than the pipe itself, which is absolutely vital for pressurized hot water distribution and high-pressure industrial applications.

#### Key Applications of the K-Aqua Flange Adaptor
Thanks to the outstanding physical and chemical properties of PP-R polypropylene, the stub end is deployed across numerous diverse industries.
* **Potable Water Supply and Sanitary Plumbing:** The K-Aqua piping system is strictly non-toxic, physiologically harmless, and complies with the highest international hygiene standards. It guarantees zero alteration to the taste or odor of the drinking water.
* **Heating, Ventilation, and Air Conditioning (HVAC):** Due to the excellent natural thermal insulation properties of PP-R, the formation of external condensation is minimized, and thermal heat losses are drastically reduced.
* **Industrial Piping and Plant Engineering:** Connecting massive valves, heavy-duty pumps, and large industrial filtration units requires robust flanged connections that will not fail or fatigue, even when exposed to severe mechanical vibrations or sudden water hammer effects.
* **Marine and Offshore Installations:** Absolute resistance to rust and galvanic corrosion makes the K-Aqua system ideal for use in highly humid, saline, or aggressive maritime environments.

#### Environmental Sustainability and Acoustic Performance
* **Eco-Friendly Construction:** PP-R is a 100% recyclable thermoplastic. Its highly energy-efficient manufacturing process, combined with an exceptionally long operational lifespan of over 50 years under standard conditions, significantly reduces the carbon footprint of construction projects. 
* **Superior Sound Insulation:** The high molecular density and dampening properties of PP-R effectively absorb flow noises and hydraulic shocks, offering superior acoustic comfort compared to traditional metallic piping systems.
To assemble the connection, a matching loose backing ring (flange) is slipped over the stub end before the mating equipment is bolted securely in place.
