### Der K-Aqua PP-R Sprungbogen (Cross Over Pipe): Die ultimative Lösung für kreuzende Rohrleitungen
In der modernen Sanitär- und Heizungstechnik stoßen Installateure häufig auf eine typische Herausforderung: Rohrleitungen müssen sich kreuzen, ohne dass sich die Einbauhöhe signifikant vergrößert oder die Strömungseigenschaften negativ beeinträchtigt werden. Genau für diesen speziellen Anwendungsfall wurde der K-Aqua PP-R Sprungbogen, auch als Cross Over Pipe bekannt, entwickelt. Dieses unverzichtbare Fitting-Bauteil aus hochwertigem Polypropylen Random-Copolymer (PP-R) ermöglicht eine nahtlose, sichere und platzsparende Überkreuzung von warmen oder kalten Trinkwasser- und Heizungsleitungen.

#### Herausragende Materialeigenschaften von PP-R
Das bei der Herstellung des K-Aqua Sprungbogens verwendete Polypropylen (PP-R) zeichnet sich durch außergewöhnliche physikalische und chemische Eigenschaften aus. PP-R ist extrem korrosionsbeständig und absolut hygienisch, was es zur idealen Wahl für Trinkwasserinstallationen macht. Im Gegensatz zu metallischen Rohren gibt es hier keine Rostbildung, keine Inkrustation und somit keine Beeinträchtigung der Wasserqualität. Die glatte Innenoberfläche des Fittings sorgt für einen minimalen Reibungswiderstand, was zu einem konstanten Wasserdruck und geringen Strömungsgeräuschen führt. Darüber hinaus weist das Material eine hervorragende Temperaturbeständigkeit auf, wodurch es problemlos sowohl für Kalt- als auch für Warmwasseranwendungen eingesetzt werden kann.

### The K-Aqua PP-R Cross Over Pipe: The Ultimate Solution for Intersecting Pipelines
In modern plumbing and heating technology, installers frequently face a typical challenge: pipelines must cross each other without significantly increasing the installation height or negatively affecting flow characteristics. The K-Aqua PP-R Cross Over Pipe was developed exactly for this specific application. This indispensable fitting component, made from high-quality Polypropylene Random Copolymer (PP-R), enables a seamless, secure, and space-saving crossover of hot or cold drinking water and heating pipes.

#### Outstanding Material Properties of PP-R
The Polypropylene (PP-R) used in the manufacture of the K-Aqua Cross Over Pipe is characterized by exceptional physical and chemical properties. PP-R is extremely corrosion-resistant and absolutely hygienic, making it the ideal choice for potable water installations. Unlike metallic pipes, there is no rust formation, no incrustation, and therefore no impairment of water quality. The smooth inner surface of the fitting ensures minimal frictional resistance, leading to constant water pressure and low flow noise. Furthermore, the material exhibits excellent temperature resistance, allowing it to be used without problems for both cold and hot water applications.

#### Technical Specifications and Dimensions
To ensure maximum flexibility in pipe installation, the K-Aqua Cross Over Pipe is available in three common diameters (d): 20 mm (Article code: AQ28520), 25 mm (Article code: AQ28525), and 32 mm (Article code: AQ28532).
- Dimension 20 mm (AQ28520): With a total length L1 of 54 mm, L2 of 53 mm, and a bow height (H) of 36.5 mm, this fitting is perfectly suited for standard potable water lines in single-family homes. The low weight of only 0.06 kg greatly facilitates handling. Packaging unit: 100 pieces.
- Dimension 25 mm (AQ28525): This model offers a length L1 of 42 mm with a height of 37.0 mm. Weighing 0.09 kg, it is more robust and optimal for main distribution lines. Packaging unit: 70 pieces.
- Dimension 32 mm (AQ28532): The largest version with L1 54 mm, L2 68 mm, and height 37.0 mm weighs 0.15 kg and is used in systems with higher flow requirements, such as in apartment buildings or commercial properties. Packaging unit: 50 pieces.

#### Diverse Application Areas
The primary purpose of the PP-R Cross Over Pipe is for routing pipes in confined spaces. Particularly when retrofitting radiators, underfloor heating systems, or in complex sanitary installations in pre-wall elements, crossing pipes can often not be avoided. With the Cross Over Pipe, the crossing line is routed elegantly and with optimized flow around the obstacle (the other pipeline).
Typical areas of application include:
- Potable Water Installations: For cold and hot water distribution in residential and commercial buildings; completely food-safe and odorless.
- Heating Systems: Ideal for connecting radiators and heating circuit distributors, as the PP-R material is extremely heat-resistant and pressure-resistant.
- Air Conditioning and Cooling Systems: Thanks to the excellent insulating properties of PP-R, condensation formation is minimized, making the crossover pipe predestined for chilled water systems.
- Industrial Plants: Due to its high chemical resistance, the fitting is also suitable for transporting compressed air and various chemical media (following a resistance check).

#### Advantages of the K-Aqua Connection Technology
The installation of the crossover pipe is carried out using the proven polyfusion welding (socket welding) method. In this process, the pipe and fitting are briefly heated and then pushed into each other. The material fuses into an inseparable, homogeneous unit. This connection technology is extremely secure, permanently leak-proof, and requires no additional sealing materials such as O-rings or hemp, which could wear out over time. The installation effort is significantly reduced, saving time and costs on the construction site.

Another decisive factor when choosing the K-Aqua PP-R Cross Over Pipe is its extraordinary longevity. Under normal operating conditions, the system has a lifespan of 50 years or more. PP-R is also an environmentally friendly material that is easy to recycle. Due to the low heat loss of the material, the overall system contributes to increasing the energy efficiency of buildings, which is an immense advantage given today's sustainability requirements. Trust in K-Aqua for a future-proof, leak-free, and highly professional pipe installation.
