Installateure stehen regelmäßig vor dem Problem, dass sich Wasser- oder Heizungsrohre kreuzen müssen, ohne dass dabei eine hydraulische Verbindung entsteht oder viel Platz in der Höhe beansprucht wird. #### Herausragende Materialeigenschaften des Polypropylen Random-Copolymer (PP-R)

Der K-Aqua Sprungbogen mit Muffe wird aus hochwertigem Polypropylen Random-Copolymer (PP-R) hergestellt. Dieses Material hat die Kunststoffrohrtechnik revolutioniert. Einer der größten Vorteile von PP-R ist seine absolute Korrosionsbeständigkeit. Anders als bei metallischen Leitungssystemen wie Kupfer oder verzinktem Stahl gibt es hier keinen Rost und keine Inkrustationen. Das bedeutet, dass die Innenseite des Fittings über Jahrzehnte hinweg glatt bleibt, was den Reibungswiderstand des Wassers drastisch reduziert. Dies führt zu einem minimalen Druckverlust und einer deutlich ruhigeren Wasserströmung. Zudem ist PP-R chemisch äußerst widerstandsfähig und absolut hygienisch, wodurch es uneingeschränkt für Trinkwasserinstallationen zugelassen ist. Es ist lebensmittelecht und verändert weder den Geschmack noch den Geruch des Wassers.

#### Effiziente Montage durch Polyfusionsschweißung

Der größte Vorteil dieses speziellen Fittings liegt in den integrierten Muffen. Nach dem Erkalten verschmelzen die Kunststoffketten zu einer absolut homogenen und dauerhaft dichten Einheit. ### The K-Aqua PP-R Cross Over with Socket: The Intelligent Solution for Crossing Pipelines

In modern plumbing, heating, and air conditioning technology, routing pipes in extremely tight spaces often presents a massive challenge. Installers regularly face the problem of water or heating pipes having to cross each other without creating a hydraulic connection or taking up too much vertical space. The K-Aqua PP-R Cross Over with Socket is designed exactly for such critical installation points. Thanks to the already integrated sockets at both ends of the arch, this fitting not only provides a flow-optimized crossover but also saves a massive amount of assembly time, as no separate sockets need to be welded on.

#### Outstanding Material Properties of Polypropylene Random Copolymer (PP-R)

The K-Aqua Cross Over with Socket is manufactured from premium Polypropylene Random Copolymer (PP-R). This material has revolutionized plastic pipe technology. One of the greatest advantages of PP-R is its absolute corrosion resistance. Unlike metallic piping systems such as copper or galvanized steel, there is no rust and no incrustation here. This means that the inside of the fitting remains smooth for decades, drastically reducing the frictional resistance of the water. This results in minimal pressure loss and a significantly quieter water flow. In addition, PP-R is highly chemically resistant and absolutely hygienic, making it fully approved for drinking water installations. It is food-safe and does not alter the taste or smell of the water.

#### Technical Specifications and Dimensions

To ensure maximum flexibility in pipe installation, the K-Aqua Cross Over with Socket is available in the most common diameters. These include 20 mm, 25 mm, and 32 mm, covering almost all standard applications in residential and commercial construction.

1. Dimension 20 mm (Article Code: AQ29520): This size is ideal for end distribution in single-family homes or for the direct connection of individual consumers. The integrated sockets ensure fast processing for standard potable water lines.
2. Dimension 25 mm (Article Code: AQ29525): The 25 mm version is particularly suitable for distribution lines or riser pipes where a higher water flow is required. Here, installers benefit from its robustness and space-saving design.
3. Dimension 32 mm (Article Code: AQ29532): In apartment buildings, commercial facilities, or for main lines, the 32 mm version is used. It ensures an excellent volume flow with minimal pressure drop despite the crossover.

#### Diverse Application Areas in Building Services

The possible applications for the crossover with socket are extremely broad. In potable water installations, it enables the clean separation and crossing of cold and hot water pipes, for example in installation shafts or pre-wall elements. In heating systems, the fitting is indispensable when supply and return lines, such as when connecting radiators or underfloor heating manifolds, cross paths. Since PP-R has a very high temperature and pressure resistance, the crossover is perfectly equipped for these thermally demanding applications. Even in air conditioning and cooling systems, the PP-R material offers advantages due to its excellent insulating properties, as the formation of condensation is greatly reduced.

#### Efficient Installation via Polyfusion Welding

The greatest advantage of this specific fitting lies in the integrated sockets. In so-called polyfusion welding (socket fusion welding), the pipe and fitting are simultaneously heated with a welding tool and then pushed into each other. Since the sockets are already part of the crossover arch, an entire welding process is eliminated compared to a conventional crossover pipe onto which sockets must first be placed. After cooling, the plastic chains fuse into an absolutely homogeneous and permanently tight unit. No additional seals are required, which reduces the probability of leaks to zero.

With an expected lifespan of over 50 years, the K-Aqua PP-R system represents a sustainable and secure investment. The K-Aqua PP-R Cross Over with Socket combines intelligent design with superior material properties and a time-saving connection technology. For any professional installer who values safety, efficiency, and longevity, this fitting is the perfect choice for managing complex pipe crossings.
